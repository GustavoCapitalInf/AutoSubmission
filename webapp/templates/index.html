Analysis settings

These change how results are shown and exported. Saved statements update immediately; nothing is reprocessed.

Off (default): the NSF count is the number of days that end with a negative balance among the days the statement shows a balance for: days with activity, or days the bank printed a daily balance.
On: every calendar day counts, and a day with no activity carries the previous day's ending balance.
Example: the 10th ends at −$10, nothing posts on the 11th, the 12th ends at $20. Off counts 1 negative day; on counts 2.
