Settings

These change how results are shown and exported. Saved statements update immediately; nothing is reprocessed.

Appearance

Analysis

Off (default): count the days that end negative among the days the statement shows a balance for, days with activity or days the bank printed. On: every calendar day counts, and a day with no activity carries the previous day's ending balance.

Example
NSF count for these days: 1

Mark Zelle Payments as True Revenue

Yes (default): Zelle deposits count in True revenue, flagged Zelle, with the person-to-person checks: Zelle from the business owner's own name, the same round amount repeated by one sender, and the other person-to-person checks (for example a sender the business also pays) are held for review; refunds, returns, loan money and asset sales stay out. No: every Zelle deposit is non-revenue, and its total is shown so you can compare True revenue with the Monthly Statements table. This changes True revenue, the review decision and the withholding rate on every statement and package analysed since this setting was added; nothing is reprocessed. An older analysis says so on its True card.

Flagged positions

A ticked group is flagged on statements and packages: listed in the Flagged positions panel, tagged in the transactions and written to the downloads. A flagged group never counts as a lender and never changes withholding or the package decision; unticking it only hides it.

Statements

For statements that hold several accounts, list every account with its own transactions and average daily balance. Off: only the main account is shown, the business checking account with the most deposits. Saved in this browser only.

Total monthly deposits: everything going in, transfers from other accounts and lender funding included. True revenue: every posted credit except known non-revenue (own-account transfers, funding, refunds) and the deposits held for review. The verified part, with positive proof, is shaded inside it on the monthly chart. Both shows the two side by side. The monthly chart follows the same choice, and a statement whose revenue could not be established shows N/A. Every choice also shows each statement's confirmed withholding rate. Saved in this browser only.
